# ZIXDR SOCIAL

Your People. Your Vibe. Your ZIXDR.

ZIXDR SOCIAL is an independent web-based social networking platform created by Rohit.

## Official Website

https://zixdr.vercel.app

## What is ZIXDR?

ZIXDR is a social platform where people can connect and share content.

## Features

- Create and share posts
- Like posts
- Comment on posts
- Repost content
- Send messages
